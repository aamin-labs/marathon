# How the book's tables were turned into single numbers:
# - Where the book gives a range (6–10 km, 4–5 reps, "Off or 6–8 km"), one value was chosen so the
#   week lands near the Breeze 80 goal mileage shown above each week. Change any of them to suit the day.
# - "% 5k" paces need your current 5K time in Setup. A bare % is of marathon pace.
# - Strides are written as 20 s fast, 40 s easy. The book gives only the 20 s; the 40 s is an assumption.
# - "Walk/jog" recoveries are written as easy jogging.
# The book's own tables, with their ranges, are in breeze.md next to this file.
